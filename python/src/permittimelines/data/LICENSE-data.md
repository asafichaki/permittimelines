Original sources, all public record:

- City of Los Angeles Building Permits (LADBS), datasets `pi9x-tg5x` and
  `gwh9-jnip` on data.lacity.org.
- City of San Diego Development Permits (DSD), development-permits-set1 on
  data.sandiego.gov.
- San Francisco Building Permits (DBI), dataset `i98e-djp9` on data.sfgov.org.
- City of Seattle Building Permits (SDCI), dataset `76t5-zqzr` on
  data.seattle.gov.

Prepared version: Renology, Residential Garage-Conversion and ADU Permit
Timelines, version 2026.1, retrieved 2026-08-24.

No city endorses this preparation. The prepared files contain no names,
addresses, coordinates, or free-text work descriptions; only public permit
identifiers, dates, and categorical fields are retained.
